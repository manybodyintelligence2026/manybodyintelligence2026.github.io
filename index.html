Many-Body Intelligence

Materials Theory division · Department of Physics and Astronomy · Uppsala University

A one-day hackathon on using AI in materials theory research.

Small teams from across the division each pick a research problem none of them has worked on, and spend the day seeing how far they can get with it using AI assistants. The day ends with a five-minute talk from each team.

You don't need a team or an idea to sign up. Everyone in the division is welcome, whatever your career stage and however much you have used these tools. We will email everyone who signs up with the details.

What to expect

  • A day working in a small team, ideally with people from groups you don't usually work with.
  • A small, real research problem to try AI assistants on, and practice at checking what they give you.
  • Tricks to pick up from colleagues: code, derivations, literature searches, figures and animations. We will share guides and example prompts before the day.
  • A five-minute talk from each team at the end, and feedback from the other teams.
  • If you want, co-authorship on the event paper, a short paper about the day.

Pizza and fika are provided. Bring a laptop.

Questions are answered in the FAQ. The code of conduct explains how we handle authorship, results and data.

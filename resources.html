Resources

Guides, Claude Code skills, starter templates, and slides for Monday 30 November, organised by phase of the day.

Before the day

Setup guide & Onboarding slides Before 30 Nov

Get Discord, Claude Code, Python, and your team's GitHub repository working, agree on a research idea in our-idea/, and try one practice log entry (/log-entry).

On the day (by phase)

1 · Scope Coming soon 09:15–10:15

Sharpen the research question, run a focused literature check, decide what “done” means by 17:00, and split tasks. Your team repository already includes the /scope-to-one-day skill.

2 · First attempt & First result Coming soon 10:15–12:15

Get a first derivation or calculation running, coordinate parallel Claude Code sessions across your team's repository, and record tasks and milestones with /log-entry and /stage.

3 · Check Coming soon 13:00–14:00

Run independent cross-checks (limiting cases, symmetries, units, convergence, analytic vs. numerical, or a second Claude session trying to break the result) and record them in checks/checks.md.

4 · Figures & presentation Available 14:00–15:00

Five Claude Code skills (/figure-style, /schematic, /render-and-look, /html-animation, /blender-scene), starter templates, interactive examples, and copy-and-paste prompts for your 2-page note and 15:45 lightning talk.

Open Figures & presentation guide → Talk slides (12 slides) Skills pack (.zip)
5 · Write Coming soon 15:00–15:45

Write the 2-page note in note/note.md (question, method, checked results, AI-use record, open issues), run /log-check, and fill in CONTRIBUTIONS.md.

6 · Lightning talks & Peer review Coming soon 15:45–17:00

Five-minute talk per team (slides in talk/), followed by swapping notes with another team and completing the referee form in review/.

Where skills live

Bookkeeping and scoping skills (/scope-to-one-day, /log-entry, /log-check, /stage) are already installed in your team repository's .claude/skills/ folder — leave that folder as it is. Optional personal skills (such as the five figure skills above) go in your personal ~/.claude/skills/ folder.
